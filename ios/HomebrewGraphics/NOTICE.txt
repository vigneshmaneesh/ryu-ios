Adapted for Ryu iOS: Ryu Catch game logic, 640x360 dynamic rendering, guest HID controls, and guest-generated PCM audio. The game uses deko3d and libnx; their licenses are included with the app.
No Nintendo code, firmware, keys or game assets are included.
